# Message image brief: images for ten next-step cards

You create ten images for Rock Solid Church's next-step messages in Rock RMS sample data. You are the image model: read the files named here, make each image, and save it. Everything you need is filled in below. Read this whole page before you start.

Images 1 to 9 are delivered, on branch `codex/message-card-images`, and this page records them as made. Make image 10, and remake an earlier one only when Jon asks.

Work in your own branch from `main`, in the `rock-sample-data` repository. All paths are from the root of the repository. This is the same kind of work as `photos/event-brief.md`, and the same rules apply where this page does not say otherwise.

## Where the images show

Rock shows each signed-in person a few "next step" cards on the church website's homepage. Each card has an image on top, then the card's title, a sentence, and a button. Rock picks the card that fits the person. A visitor who is not signed in sees each message's last card, such as "Plan a Visit" and "Ways to Give".

Fifteen cards already use approved event images from `photos/events/`. These ten get their own. Look at a few event images first, such as `photos/events/group-leader-training.jpg` and `photos/events/baptism-sunday.jpg`, so the new images sit well beside them.

## Image size

Every image is **2:1 landscape**, the same as the event images, because the cards sit side by side.

1. Make the image in landscape, as large as your tool allows. If your tool makes 3:2 (for example 1536 by 1024), keep the subject in the middle band.
2. Deliver a JPEG of exactly **1536 by 768 pixels**. Crop the top and bottom of a 3:2 image to get 2:1. Never stretch or squash it.
3. Keep the subject away from the edges. The card is narrow, about 300 to 640 pixels wide, so the subject must read at that size.

## No text at all

The card prints its title right under the image. So **no image has any text**: no title, no words, letters, numbers, logos, or signs. That includes phone screens, cups, shirts, banners, and cards in someone's hand. This is different from the event designs, which carry a title.

## Two kinds of image

1. **Photo.** A real-looking photo, like one a church staff member took. Natural light, real places, slightly imperfect framing. Follow "The look of a photo" in `photos/event-brief.md`.
2. **Design.** A dimensional papercraft still life in the church's brand colors: warm ivory, sand, sea-glass, deep teal, and a little coral. Layered, finely textured paper with soft, real shadows, like the approved event artwork. Follow "The look of a design" in `photos/event-brief.md`, except that a design here has no title and no people's faces.

## Rules you must not break

1. **No real people.** Never use a photo of a real person as a reference. No face may look like a real or famous person.
2. **No named people.** Do not attach Cast profile photos. No one in these images is meant to be recognized.
3. **No clear child faces.**
4. **No text anywhere,** as "No text at all" says.
5. **No real places, brands, or characters.** No famous landmark, real business, logo, or car maker's design.
6. **Safe and gentle.** No money, cash, checks, or credit cards in any image. No alcohol, political symbols, or flags.
7. **Respectful images.** Follow `promptRules` in `photos/ethnicities.json`. Groups show natural variety in age, skin tone, and build.
8. **Do not change any other file.** Only add the files and the manifest named below.

## Saving the images

1. Save each full-size image to `photos/originals/messages/{key}.png` (or `.jpg`, in the format your tool made). For example, `photos/originals/messages/giving/first-gift.png`.
2. Save the delivered JPEG, 1536 by 768 pixels, quality 85, sRGB, with image metadata removed, to `photos/messages/{key}.jpg`. Keep it under 500 KB.
3. Create `photos/messages/manifest.json` with one entry per image, in the order of this page:

```json
{
  "model": "the image model you used",
  "images": [
    {
      "file": "messages/your-next-step/plan-a-visit.jpg",
      "message": "your-next-step",
      "adaptation": "plan-a-visit",
      "seed": null,
      "prompt": "the exact prompt you used"
    }
  ]
}
```

`message` and `adaptation` are the two parts of the key. `file` is the path under `photos/`.

## Check every image

Look at every image before you save it. Remake any that fail.

1. It is exactly 1536 by 768, and the subject reads at 300 pixels wide.
2. It has no text, letters, numbers, logos, or signs anywhere.
3. It is the kind its section names: a photo or a design.
4. No child's face is clear, and no adult's face is the subject.
5. No distorted faces, extra fingers, odd hands, or melted objects.
6. The Plan a Visit entrance matches the reference image.
7. No two images look alike, and they sit well beside the event images.
