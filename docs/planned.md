# Planned work

What we plan to add to Rock Sample Data but have not built yet. If something you need is missing, or you have an idea, see [Contributing](contributing.md) to propose it. For what is already done, see [How it works](how-it-works.md) and [Meet the Cast](../cast/families/README.md).

## Groups and serving

- **Women's groups,** led by women, with a women's Bible study topic.
- **Group leader requirements,** such as a background check, with a few leaders whose check is missing or expired so Rock shows the warning.

## People and attendance

- **Realistic names for the generated crowd,** drawn from public name frequency data by birth decade.
- **Check-in relationships,** such as a grandparent who may check in a grandchild, loaded from the Cast's known relationships.
- **More Cast families** whenever a test or demo needs a case the Cast does not cover yet.

## Giving

- **Refunds** and other uncommon transactions.

## Other Rock features

- **Learning (LMS):** learning programs, courses, and classes, with people enrolled, activities completed, and program completions, so Rock's learning screens have real data.
- **Event registrations,** such as camps and classes, with fees, discounts, and placement groups.
- **Communications:** email, text, and push history, with very large mass emails, opens and clicks, SMS conversations with real back-and-forth threads, and messages tied to steps. See [Communications](communications.md).
- **Benevolence requests.**
- **Reminders** that staff would really set.

- **The church's app:** pages and content for the Rock Solid Church App, which starts as a stub for push notifications.

## Installing and quality

- **Install from inside Rock.** Choose a dataset in Rock and install it, instead of running a separate tool.
- **Published packages.** Each release builds the datasets and publishes them automatically, so Rock always installs a tested version.
- **Closer to real churches.** Today 89% of measured values fall within tolerance of real church patterns. The goal is 90% or more.
